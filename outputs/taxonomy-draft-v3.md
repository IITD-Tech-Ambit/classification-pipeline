## Global classifier rules (applied across all themes)

1. METHOD vs APPLICATION (operational test). Assign a paper to an 'AI/ML, Supercomputing & Quantum Computing' domain ONLY if its novelty is a new or modified algorithm/architecture, or a general methodological result on benchmark-style data. If it uses standard/off-the-shelf methods (SVM, HMM, standard CNN, a standard CFD solver, kriging, etc.) to obtain a result about a specific application (a disease, a communication channel, a road network, a flood), assign the APPLICATION domain instead. For medical signals/images prefer Healthcare & MedTech; for a named engineering application prefer that theme's domain. Motivation/keyword is not enough — classify by the primary scientific contribution.

2. OUT-OF-SCOPE ROUTING. If a paper is genuinely out of scope for all 9 themes -- e.g. pure/fundamental physics (dark matter, gravitational waves, astrochemistry, cosmology, continuum/second-gradient elasticity theory with no engineering target), pure mathematics or cryptography theory, or internal-combustion-engine combustion with no better home -- do NOT force it into a least-bad domain. Set unclassifiable=true with reason beginning 'out_of_scope:' and a short justification. Reserve this for genuine no-home papers, not merely difficult ones.

## Rules of the taxonomy

- **Two levels**: 9 fixed thematic areas -> 5–10 domains each. Each domain belongs to exactly one theme.
- **Single label**; **no duplicate domain names** across themes; cross-theme calls settled by the tie-break rules in each scope note and the global rules above.

## 1. AI/ML, Supercomputing & Quantum Computing  (5 domains)

**Scope.** This theme encompasses foundational and applied research in artificial intelligence, machine learning, high-performance computing, and quantum computing. Core domains include machine learning algorithms and neural network architectures (from feature selection to deep learning), natural language processing and information retrieval systems (text analysis, entity resolution, summarization), theoretical computer science (algorithm design, complexity analysis, graph and parallel algorithms), and mathematical foundations for dynamical systems (control theory, Lyapunov stability, system identification). Also included are computational methods for solving differential equations and simulating complex physical phenomena, particularly in fluid mechanics and flow analysis. Papers in this theme typically present advances in learning algorithms, algorithmic techniques, NLP methods, or mathematical frameworks for analyzing dynamical systems—such as novel neural network architectures for color quantization, VC dimension bounds, latent Dirichlet allocation for selectional preferences, Lyapunov function computation, or numerical schemes for compressible flows. Explicitly excluded are: quantum devices, quantum materials, and semiconductor fabrication (assign to Quantum Technologies & Semiconductor Technology); biomaterial synthesis and characterization not tied to a specific clinical application (assign to Advanced Materials & Devices); and wireless communication systems, signal modulation, and electromagnetic wave propagation (assign to Next-Gen Communication). When a paper addresses quantum algorithms, quantum information theory, or quantum computing as a computational paradigm, it belongs in this theme; conversely, quantum photonic devices and quantum material systems belong in Quantum Technologies. When applying machine learning methods to a clinical problem, classify by the primary contribution: if the advance is in the learning algorithm itself, assign to this theme; if the advance is in medical imaging, diagnostics, or clinical application, assign to Healthcare & MedTech.

| Domain | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Machine Learning & Neural Networks** | Core ML algorithms, neural network architectures, feature selection, kernel methods, and learning theory. Includes supervised and unsupervised learning techniques. |
| **Natural Language Processing & Information Retrieval** | NLP tasks including text summarization, entity resolution, selectional preferences, fact-checking, and graph-based information retrieval methods. |
| **Algorithms & Computational Complexity** | Algorithm design, complexity analysis, graph algorithms, parallel algorithms, and optimization methods for computational problems.  SCOPE GUARD: this domain is for CS algorithm/complexity contributions (data structures, graph/parallel algorithms, complexity bounds, optimization methods). It is NOT a home for physics, continuum/structural mechanics, or hardware-layout papers; such papers with no CS-algorithmic contribution and no other theme fit -> unclassifiable (out_of_scope). TIE-BREAK: an algorithm whose contribution is a networking/routing protocol -> Next-Gen Communication / Wireless Network Access & Resource Allocation; a graph method whose contribution is social-network insight -> Social Sciences, Humanities & Management / Social Media Analytics & Online Networks; only application-agnostic algorithm design stays here. |
| **Control Systems & Linear Dynamical Systems** | Theory and algorithms for linear time-invariant systems, adaptive control, system identification, observer design, and Lyapunov stability analysis. |
| **Computational Fluid Dynamics & Flow Analysis** | Numerical methods for simulating fluid flows, turbulence modeling, stability analysis of flows, and solutions to PDEs in fluid mechanics. |

## 2. Advanced Materials & Devices  (9 domains)

**Scope.** Advanced Materials & Devices encompasses the discovery, synthesis, characterization, and fundamental property engineering of new and engineered materials across polymers, metals, ceramics, composites, and nanomaterials, spanning from bio-based polymers and sustainable synthesis methods to ferroelectric ceramics, rare-earth phosphors, and thin-film nanostructures. This theme covers microstructural analysis, structure-property relationships, mechanical and thermal behavior, optical and magnetic properties, and materials design—including examples such as green synthesis of bio-based nanoparticles, grain boundary behavior in metallic alloys, ion-beam modification of thin films, dielectric properties of ceramic composites, and polymer nanocomposite characterization via spectroscopy. Materials processing and joining techniques (machining, welding, additive manufacturing) belong instead to Manufacturing & Industry 4.0, while materials engineered for specific clinical or therapeutic applications (drug delivery vehicles, tissue scaffolds, diagnostic biosensors) go to Healthcare & MedTech. Biomaterials designed for general use without a targeted therapeutic application—such as cellulose-based foams or chitosan nanoparticles for adsorption or multifunctional characteristics—remain in Advanced Materials & Devices. If a paper's primary contribution is a quantum device, semiconductor fabrication process, or photodetection system, assign it to Quantum Technologies & Semiconductor Technology; if the contribution is quantum algorithms or quantum information science, assign it to AI/ML, Supercomputing & Quantum Computing. Energy-storage or energy-conversion materials (batteries, photovoltaic absorbers, thermoelectric materials) belong to Energy, Sustainability & Climate Change rather than this theme. Functional/antibacterial/antimicrobial nanomaterials and nanoparticle materials studied for their material properties—even when an antibacterial, antimicrobial, or other biological assay is used to characterize the material—belong in Advanced Materials & Devices (e.g., Bio-based Materials & Polymers or the relevant materials domain), NOT in Healthcare's clinical domains. Tie-break: antibacterial/antimicrobial or bioactivity testing of a material, where the contribution is the material itself, → Advanced Materials; only assign Healthcare when there is a specific clinical/therapeutic use in patients.

| Domain | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Bio-based Materials & Polymers** | Natural and synthetic polymers, biocomposites, and bio-inspired materials derived from renewable sources including cellulose, chitosan, proteins, and plant fibers. Includes green synthesis methods and sustainable material development. |
| **Alloys & Structural Metals** | Microstructure, mechanical properties, and phase behavior of metallic alloys including high-entropy alloys, intermetallics, magnesium alloys, and related deformation mechanisms. Covers grain boundary behavior, creep, and fatigue performance. |
| **Ion Beam & Thin Film Engineering** | Synthesis and modification of thin films and nanostructures using ion irradiation, plasma processes, and high-energy particle treatments. Includes CVD, evaporation, and ion-track formation for structural and optical property engineering. |
| **Composite & Laminate Structures** | Mechanical analysis and design of composite laminates, sandwich structures, and functionally graded materials including piezoelectric composites, shell structures, and impact behavior under thermal and mechanical loading.  TIE-BREAK (mechanical/structural-analysis papers): if the object is a civil/infrastructure structure (building, bridge, tank, foundation, seismic response) -> Smart & Sustainable Infrastructure / Structural Dynamics & Seismic Engineering; if the object is a fiber-reinforced, laminated, or functionally-graded composite studied for its structural mechanics -> Advanced Materials & Devices / Composite & Laminate Structures; if the contribution is material mechanical behavior tied to a forming/joining/machining process or high-strain-rate metal response -> Manufacturing & Industry 4.0 / Materials, Joining & Mechanical Behavior. |

Covers structure-property relationships, impedance behavior, and magneto-transport phenomena.  Cement / concrete and cementitious materials do NOT belong here -> Smart & Sustainable Infrastructure / Construction Materials & Concrete Technology. |
| **Polymer Nanocomposites & Blends** | Synthesis, microstructure characterization, and properties of polymer composites with nanofillers (carbon nanotubes, graphene, nanographite) and block copolymer systems. Includes thermal, mechanical, and tribological property analysis via NMR and spectroscopy. |
| **Ionic Liquids & Soft Matter** | Solvation dynamics, self-assembly, and microstructure of ionic liquids, deep eutectic solvents, and their mixtures. Covers surfactant behavior, dye aggregation, and fluorescence studies in ionic liquid systems. |
| **Organometallic & Chalcogenide Synthesis** | Discovery and characterization of organometallic compounds and chalcogenide materials for new material platforms.  Also covers general organic / coordination-compound synthesis. TIE-BREAK (synthesis vs therapy): synthesis of small molecules / coordination compounds where the contribution is the synthetic method or the compound's material / structural characterization -> Advanced Materials & Devices / Organometallic & Chalcogenide Synthesis, EVEN when a biological activity is stated as motivation. Assign Healthcare & MedTech / Drug Delivery & Molecular Therapeutics ONLY when the paper evaluates the compound in a biological / pharmacological model (in-vitro/in-vivo efficacy, PK, or a delivery vehicle). Motivation != evaluation. |
| **Rare-Earth Doped Luminescent Materials** | Synthesis and optical characterization of rare-earth-doped phosphors and nanocrystals for photonic and lighting applications. Covers upconversion, persistent luminescence, photoluminescence, and white-LED phosphors. |

## 3. Energy, Sustainability & Climate Change  (10 domains)

**Scope.** This theme encompasses research on energy generation, conversion, storage, and distribution technologies; sustainability assessment and natural resource management; and climate dynamics and regional modeling relevant to India's energy transition and environmental challenges. Core domains include biomass and biofuel conversion from agricultural residues, solar thermal and photovoltaic energy systems, grid integration and smart microgrids for renewable energy, carbon utilization and catalytic conversion of CO₂ and biomass, environmental assessment and land-use management, monsoon dynamics and regional climate modeling, and electric vehicle battery and charging systems. Research on the physics, chemistry, and engineering of energy materials and devices belongs here—such as perovskite solar cells, iron-cerium catalysts for hydrogen production, or battery management systems—whereas foundational discovery, synthesis, and characterization of novel materials without a specific energy application goes to Advanced Materials & Devices. Energy-efficient building design, urban energy systems, and smart grid infrastructure as critical infrastructure belong to Smart & Sustainable Infrastructure, not here. Machine learning applications to energy systems or climate modeling belong in AI/ML, Supercomputing & Quantum Computing if the methodological contribution (algorithm, model architecture) is the primary focus; conversely, if a paper develops a neural network model specifically to predict solar thermal performance or grid control, it is assigned here because the energy engineering problem is primary. Papers on agricultural sustainability, soil health, ecosystem services, and environmental remediation are included when linked to energy, land use, or climate; general soil mechanics or wastewater treatment as infrastructure belongs to Smart & Sustainable Infrastructure. Climate simulation, monsoon dynamics, and atmospheric modeling using regional climate models and satellite data are central to this theme, whereas broader atmospheric physics or plasma-electromagnetic phenomena unrelated to climate belong elsewhere.

| Domain | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Biomass & Biofuel Technologies** | Conversion of agricultural and forestry residues into energy and fuels, including biomass gasification, bioethanol production, and sustainable bioenergy systems for addressing agricultural waste and energy security. |
| **Solar Thermal Energy & Heat Transfer Systems** | Design and optimization of solar thermal collectors and heat transfer systems for thermal energy capture and conversion.  Do NOT use as a catch-all for thermodynamic-cycle / heat-engine / combustion / energy-harvesting papers -- those go to Thermal Systems, Engines & Energy Harvesting. |
| **Next-Generation Photovoltaic Materials** | Development and characterization of advanced photovoltaic materials and devices including perovskites, dye-sensitized cells, organic solar cells, and nanostructured semiconductors for improved solar energy conversion efficiency. |

TIE-BREAK (Grid Integration & Smart Microgrids vs Power Systems & Grid Control): if the subject is a renewable/DER source being interfaced to the grid (PV/wind/battery inverter control, MPPT, droop control of DER, renewable microgrids) -> Energy / Grid Integration & Smart Microgrids. If the subject is the bulk power network itself, independent of any specific renewable source (load-flow, transmission loss, AGC/frequency & voltage control of interconnected grids, generator/machine modeling, protection, network stability) -> Smart & Sustainable Infrastructure / Power Systems & Grid Control. Rule of thumb: 'renewable source -> grid' = Energy; 'grid/network operation' = Infrastructure.  Do NOT use as a catch-all for thermodynamic-cycle / heat-engine / combustion / energy-harvesting papers -- those go to Thermal Systems, Engines & Energy Harvesting. |

The contribution is the storage material itself (its composition, structure, capacity, cycling, and charge-storage mechanism), NOT charger circuits or power-electronics systems (those are 'Electric Vehicle Battery & Charging Systems') and NOT renewable-source power distribution or battery-in-grid control (that is 'Grid Integration & Smart Microgrids'). |

Prefer this domain over Grid Integration & Smart Microgrids or Solar Thermal Energy & Heat Transfer Systems for heat-engine, refrigeration and energy-harvesting papers. ## 4. Healthcare & MedTech  (10 domains)

**Scope.** Healthcare & MedTech encompasses the design, development, and clinical application of therapeutic agents, medical devices, diagnostic systems, and regenerative constructs aimed at disease prevention, detection, and treatment in human health. This includes drug delivery systems and molecular therapeutics (e.g., engineered nanoparticles with platinum cargo for cancer treatment), clinical AI and medical imaging analytics for diagnosis and decision support, tissue engineering and biomaterial scaffolds for wound healing and organ repair, biomechanical analysis of human tissues and musculoskeletal systems, biomarkers and point-of-care diagnostics, cellular and molecular investigations of disease mechanisms, biosensors for clinical monitoring, infectious disease pathogenesis, biopharmaceutical manufacturing and quality control, and neuroscience research on neural signaling and neurological conditions. When a biomaterial or scaffold is designed and characterized specifically for a clinical or therapeutic application (e.g., agar-gelatin scaffolds for tissue engineering, composite scaffolds for hemostasis), it belongs in Healthcare & MedTech; discovery and synthesis of general-purpose biomaterials without explicit therapeutic intent belong in Advanced Materials & Devices instead. When machine learning or AI methods are applied to medical imaging, clinical decision-making, or disease prediction in a healthcare context, the work belongs here; if the primary contribution is a novel ML algorithm or computational method independent of healthcare application, it belongs in AI/ML, Supercomputing & Quantum Computing instead. Wearable sensors and biosensors developed for clinical monitoring (e.g., wrap-around coils for joint flexion monitoring) are included here, whereas general sensor device physics or semiconductor fabrication belongs in Quantum Technologies & Semiconductor Technology. Biomanufacturing and fermentation processes directed toward biopharmaceutical production belong here, while general fermentation and bioprocess engineering belong in Manufacturing & Industry 4.0.

| Domain | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Drug Delivery & Molecular Therapeutics** | Design, synthesis, and evaluation of drug delivery systems, nanoparticles, and molecular compounds for therapeutic applications, including computational drug design, ligand binding, and pharmacokinetics. |
| **Clinical AI & Medical Imaging Analytics** | Application of machine learning, deep learning, and artificial intelligence algorithms for medical image analysis, disease detection, diagnosis support, and clinical decision-making systems. |
| **Tissue Engineering & Regenerative Medicine** | Fabrication and characterization of bioactive scaffolds, hydrogels, and biomaterial constructs for tissue regeneration, wound healing, and organ repair. |
| **Biomechanics & Musculoskeletal Systems** | Mechanical analysis of human tissues, joints, ligaments, and musculoskeletal structures under various loading conditions, including wearable sensors and impact biomechanics. |
| **Clinical Diagnostics & Biomarkers** | Development and validation of non-invasive diagnostic methods, biomarkers, clinical assessments, and physiological measurements for disease detection and monitoring. |
| **Cellular & Molecular Biology** | Investigation of intracellular signaling pathways, protein function, gene expression, metabolic processes, and molecular mechanisms underlying disease and cellular responses. |
| **Clinical Biosensors & Point-of-Care Devices** | Development of biosensing platforms and portable diagnostic instruments for clinical and bedside applications. |
| **Infectious Disease & Microbiology** | Molecular pathogenesis, immunological responses, genetic characterization, and functional studies of pathogenic microorganisms including tuberculosis and other infections. |
| **Biopharmaceutical Manufacturing & Quality** | Analytical methods, characterization, stability assessment, and quality control of biopharmaceutical products including monoclonal antibodies, biosimilars, and recombinant proteins. |
| **Neuroscience & Neurophysiology** | Cellular, molecular and systems neuroscience and neurophysiology, including neural signalling, brain injury and neurological-condition mechanisms, neural rehabilitation and neuromuscular assessment. |

## 5. Manufacturing & Industry 4.0  (9 domains)

**Scope.** Manufacturing & Industry 4.0 encompasses the design, control, optimization, and characterization of industrial production processes and equipment, spanning electrical drives and power conversion, rotating machinery dynamics, precision machining and material joining, surface finishing, multiphase flow systems, and biomanufacturing. The theme covers experimental and computational studies of friction, wear, lubrication, vibration analysis, material mechanical behavior under manufacturing conditions, textile production, and fermentation scaling—as exemplified by research on harmonic mitigation in motor drives, bearing performance with nano-lubricants, hot embossing for microstructures, ultrasonic welding of dissimilar joints, yarn and fabric characterization, rotor dynamics diagnosis, and particle-laden flow manipulation. Explicitly excluded: discovery, synthesis, or fundamental characterization of new materials not tied to a specific manufacturing process belongs to Advanced Materials & Devices; energy generation, storage, or conversion technologies belong to Energy, Sustainability & Climate Change, while energy-efficient buildings and smart grids as infrastructure belong to Smart & Sustainable Infrastructure; CFD as a general computational method for non-manufacturing applications belongs to AI/ML, Supercomputing & Quantum Computing; and control systems theory in isolation belongs to AI/ML. Tie-break rule: if a paper's primary contribution is a processing or joining technique applied to materials, or characterization of material behavior during manufacturing, assign it here; if the primary contribution is synthesis or discovery of a new material or material class itself, assign it to Advanced Materials & Devices. Similarly, if research on bearings, shafts, or rotating equipment focuses on production, assembly, or operational diagnostics in industrial context, it belongs here; if it is fundamental materials science of bearing alloys, assign to Advanced Materials & Devices.

| Domain | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Electric Motor Drives & Power Electronics** | Design, control, and optimization of induction motor drives with power electronic converters, including harmonic mitigation, inverter topologies, and vector control techniques for industrial applications. |
| **Tribology & Bearing Performance** | Experimental and theoretical studies of friction, wear, lubrication, and bearing behavior including nano-lubricants, greases, micro-texturing, and brake material performance. |
| **Precision Manufacturing & Microfabrication** | Advanced manufacturing processes for creating micro and nanostructures, including hot embossing, chemical mechanical polishing, laser processing, and additive manufacturing at small scales. |
| **Materials, Joining & Mechanical Behavior** | Characterization of material properties, mechanical testing, welding processes, composite fabrication, and structural analysis under various loading conditions and manufacturing techniques.  TIE-BREAK (mechanical/structural-analysis papers): if the object is a civil/infrastructure structure (building, bridge, tank, foundation, seismic response) -> Smart & Sustainable Infrastructure / Structural Dynamics & Seismic Engineering; if the object is a fiber-reinforced, laminated, or functionally-graded composite studied for its structural mechanics -> Advanced Materials & Devices / Composite & Laminate Structures; if the contribution is material mechanical behavior tied to a forming/joining/machining process or high-strain-rate metal response -> Manufacturing & Industry 4.0 / Materials, Joining & Mechanical Behavior. |
| **Textile Manufacturing & Fabric Properties** | Production and characterization of yarns and fabrics including fiber properties, fabric mechanics, comfort characteristics, and textile processing technologies. |
| **Rotor Dynamics & Vibration Analysis** | Dynamic modeling, vibration analysis, and fault diagnosis of rotating machinery including rotors, shafts, bearings, and gearboxes in industrial equipment. |
| **Fluid-Particle Flow & Multiphase Systems** | Computational and experimental study of multiphase flows including slurries, particulate flows, fluidization, and mixing in industrial piping and processing equipment. |
| **Advanced Surface Finishing & Processing** | Precision surface finishing techniques including magnetic abrasive finishing, ultrasonic-assisted processes, EDM, ion beam milling, and additive manufacturing for surface quality enhancement. |
| **Biomanufacturing & Fermentation Processes** | Scaling, optimization, and control of biological production processes including fermentation for biopharmaceutical and biochemical manufacturing. |

## 6. Next-Gen Communication  (8 domains)

**Scope.** Next-Gen Communication encompasses the physical layer, signal processing, and network architecture of wireless and optical communication systems. This includes antenna and RF design for microwave and millimeter-wave frequencies; signal modulation, detection, and equalization techniques; beamforming and direction-of-arrival estimation using array signal processing; cooperative and relay-based multi-hop networks; free-space optical links addressing fading and atmospheric effects; microwave photonic subsystems for RF signal processing; MAC protocols and resource allocation in wireless networks; and nonlinear electromagnetic-plasma interactions for wave and wakefield generation. Papers addressing the design of beamforming algorithms, performance analysis of relay networks, atmospheric turbulence mitigation in FSO systems, Brillouin-based photonic filters, QPSK detection schemes, channel access delay modeling, and plasma-wave physics all belong here. Excluded are: machine learning methods for communication (route to AI/ML, Supercomputing & Quantum Computing); quantum algorithms and quantum information processing (route to AI/ML); quantum photonic devices and semiconductor fabrication (route to Quantum Technologies & Semiconductor Technology); and general information management or telecommunications policy (route to Social Sciences, Humanities & Management). If a paper's primary contribution is a learning algorithm or AI method applied to communication, assign it to AI/ML; if it concerns quantum devices, quantum materials, or semiconductor physics, assign it to Quantum Technologies. Plasma-wave and electromagnetic-plasma physics remains in Next-Gen Communication.

| Domain | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Direction of Arrival & Beamforming** | Signal processing techniques for estimating source directions and spatial filtering using antenna arrays, including DOA estimation, beamforming algorithms, and array signal processing methods. |
| **Cooperative & Relay Networks** | Multi-hop communication systems using relays and device-to-device networks, including cooperative multiplexing, relay selection, and performance analysis of relay-aided transmission schemes. |
| **Free Space Optical Communication** | Line-of-sight optical wireless communication systems addressing atmospheric turbulence, pointing errors, fading channels, and modulation schemes for FSO links. |
| **Microwave Photonic Systems** | Photonic devices and circuits for microwave signal processing, including Brillouin scattering-based filters, RF photonic components, and integrated photonic subsystems. |
| **Signal Modulation, Detection & Channel Equalization** | Modulation schemes, receiver detection algorithms, and equalization techniques for wireless and wired communication systems. |
| **Wireless Network Access & Resource Allocation** | MAC protocols, channel access mechanisms, frequency allocation, traffic modeling, and resource scheduling for wireless and cellular networks. |
| **Antenna Design & RF Systems** | Design and development of antennas for microwave and millimeter-wave applications, including frequency reconfigurable antennas, patch antennas, and antenna arrays. |
| **Plasma-Wave & Electromagnetic Interactions** | Nonlinear interaction of electromagnetic waves and intense laser beams with plasma media: plasma-wave and wakefield generation, self-focusing, filamentation, solitons and harmonic generation in plasma-filled structures. |

## 7. Quantum Technologies & Semiconductor Technology  (9 domains)

**Scope.** This theme encompasses the design, fabrication, characterization, and device physics of quantum and semiconductor technologies, spanning from ultrascale analog circuit design in nanoscale CMOS to advanced photonic and spintronic devices. Core research includes ultra-low voltage analog integrated circuits, semiconductor photovoltaic and photodetection devices, resistive memory and neuromorphic computing hardware, quantum light generation and integrated quantum photonic processors, structured light and polarization engineering, spintronics and magnetic devices, fiber photonic systems, wide bandgap semiconductors, and plasmonic and fiber optic sensors. The theme focuses on the physical mechanisms, device-level implementations, fabrication techniques, and experimental characterization of these technologies—for example, papers on photon pair sources, magnetic tunnel junctions, RRAM-based stochastic neurons, or polarization singularity generation all belong here. Excluded from this theme are quantum algorithms, quantum information theory, and quantum computing as computation models, which belong to AI/ML, Supercomputing & Quantum Computing; general material discovery and synthesis that is not tied to a specific device or technology demonstration, which belongs to Advanced Materials & Devices; and energy generation or storage systems intended primarily for grid integration or infrastructure applications, which belong to Energy, Sustainability & Climate Change. When a paper involves quantum materials or devices (e.g., photon sources, entanglement generation, quantum memory), assign it here; when it involves quantum algorithms or information-theoretic methods for computation, assign it to the AI/ML theme. When a paper combines semiconductor physics with energy conversion (e.g., photovoltaic cell design or characterization), assign it to this theme; only advanced grid-integrated solar systems or large-scale energy infrastructure applications go to the Energy theme.

| Domain | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Ultra-Low Voltage Analog Circuits** | Design and analysis of analog integrated circuits operating at reduced supply voltages (below 1V) for nanoscale CMOS technologies, including operational amplifiers, filters, ADCs, and image sensors. |
| **Semiconductor Photovoltaic & Photodetection Devices** | Device physics, fabrication, and performance of photovoltaic cells and photodetectors based on semiconductor materials and quantum phenomena.  TIE-BREAK (PV device vs material): contribution = device physics/fabrication of the cell/detector (junction engineering, I-V, EQE, architecture) -> Quantum Technologies & Semiconductor Technology / Semiconductor Photovoltaic & Photodetection Devices; contribution = a new absorber/HTL/ETL material or conversion efficiency of the material -> Energy / Next-Generation Photovoltaic Materials. Acousto-optic (SAW/IDT) and holographic-imaging papers are NOT photodetection (route to the relevant device/circuit domain or unclassifiable); phosphor-lighting -> Advanced Materials & Devices / Rare-Earth Doped Luminescent Materials. Do NOT use this domain as a catch-all optical-device sink. |
| **Resistive Memory & Neuromorphic Computing** | Non-volatile resistive memory technologies (RRAM, OXRAM) and their applications in stochastic neural networks, in-memory computing, random number generation, and hardware learning systems. |
| **Quantum Photonics & Entanglement** | Quantum light generation, manipulation, and characterization including photon pair sources, squeezed states, entanglement, quantum memory, and integrated quantum photonic processors. |
| **Structured Light & Polarization Engineering** | Generation and characterization of structured optical fields including vortex beams, polarization singularities, Poincaré beams, and metasurface-based manipulation of light properties. |
| **Spintronics & Magnetic Devices** | Magnetic thin films, heterostructures, and devices including magnetic tunnel junctions, spin pumping, spin torques, magnetic damping, and perpendicular magnetic anisotropy for data storage and spintronic applications. |
| **Fiber Photonic Devices & Engineering** | Design and fabrication of photonic devices, waveguides, and fiber-based optical systems for quantum and classical applications. |
| **Wide Bandgap Semiconductors & Devices** | High electron mobility transistors, Schottky diodes, and nanowire devices in III-V nitrides and wide bandgap materials with analysis of transport phenomena, thermal effects, and device modeling. |
| **Plasmonic & Fiber Optic Sensors** | Optical and plasmonic sensors based on surface plasmon resonance, fiber gratings, and tapered fibers for chemical, biological, and refractive index sensing applications. |

## 8. Smart & Sustainable Infrastructure  (9 domains)

**Scope.** Smart & Sustainable Infrastructure encompasses the design, modeling, analysis, and optimization of engineered systems that support urban and regional development while minimizing environmental impact. The theme covers power systems and smart grids; geotechnical and structural engineering for foundations and dynamic systems; transportation networks and traffic management; water resources, hydrology, and wastewater treatment; building energy performance and thermal design; urban air quality monitoring; and seismic resilience of infrastructure. Papers in this theme typically address infrastructure performance, resilience, efficiency, and environmental quality at the systems or asset level—such as load flow analysis in grids, soil reinforcement behavior, traffic flow prediction, water quality simulation in rivers, thermal retrofit of buildings, or air pollutant modeling in cities. Excluded from this theme are energy generation, storage, and conversion technologies (e.g., photovoltaic materials, battery systems, biomass conversion), which belong in Energy, Sustainability & Climate Change; materials discovery and synthesis, which belong in Advanced Materials & Devices; AI/ML methods developed primarily as algorithmic contributions, which belong in AI/ML, Supercomputing & Quantum Computing; and policy, economics, or social aspects of infrastructure, which belong in Social Sciences, Humanities & Management. When a paper applies machine learning or computational methods to infrastructure problems (e.g., kriging for traffic speed estimation, machine learning for air pollutant factor analysis), assign it to Smart & Sustainable Infrastructure if the focus is on the infrastructure application and outcomes; assign it to AI/ML only if the contribution is methodological advancement in the algorithm itself. When a paper addresses energy efficiency in buildings or smart grid operations as infrastructure systems, it belongs in Smart & Sustainable Infrastructure; only assign to Energy theme if it concerns primary energy generation or storage technologies.

TIE-BREAK (Grid Integration & Smart Microgrids vs Power Systems & Grid Control): if the subject is a renewable/DER source being interfaced to the grid (PV/wind/battery inverter control, MPPT, droop control of DER, renewable microgrids) -> Energy / Grid Integration & Smart Microgrids. ## 9. Social Sciences, Humanities & Management  (10 domains)

**Scope.** This theme encompasses research in economics, management science, information systems, library science, humanities, and cultural studies—focusing on organizational behavior, institutional practice, human experience, and social outcomes rather than technological innovation per se. Included domains span digital transformation adoption in enterprises and government, information management as professional practice, supply chain and operations management, development economics and poverty analysis, ethnographic studies of religion and identity, international business strategy, agricultural and consumer economics, digital culture and user experience, computational linguistics applied to language analysis, and social media analytics. Research on economic development and welfare schemes, caste and inequality in social programs, border studies and religious conversion, cross-border acquisitions and firm strategy, library digitization projects, and supply chain coordination all exemplify the scope. Excluded: machine learning and NLP algorithms developed primarily as computational methods (go to AI/ML theme); biomaterials designed for clinical therapeutic application (go to Healthcare & MedTech); energy generation and storage technologies (go to Energy theme); and general material synthesis and characterization (go to Advanced Materials). Tie-break rule for NLP: if the primary contribution is a computational linguistics method or parsing algorithm for processing Indian or classical languages, assign to this theme's Computational Linguistics domain; if the contribution is a machine learning algorithm whose application happens to be language, assign to AI/ML. Tie-break rule for digital systems: information systems and e-governance as organizational adoption and managerial practice belong here; the underlying AI/ML methods or infrastructure systems belong in their respective themes. Tie-break rule for economics and policy: empirical analysis of welfare schemes, development outcomes, agricultural value chains, and behavioral consumer research belong here; the statistical or experimental methodology used is secondary to the social/economic subject matter.
